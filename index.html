Football for Fun & Fitness

We started FFF Phuket so that older players could keep enjoying the game they love – and to meet the visiting teams and groups who come to Phuket. Whether you're here for a week or for good, come and play with us.

How we play

Proper football, played in a friendly spirit. Here's what to expect.

  • 7-a-side

    Seven players per team on the pitch – plenty of touches of the ball for everyone.

  • Rolling substitutions

    Come off for a breather and go back on whenever you're ready.

  • With a goalkeeper

    Each team plays with a goalkeeper.

  • No slide tackles

    We keep it safe, so everyone can play again next week.

  • Our squad

    Mainly players in their 60s, plus two players over 50 and two over 70.

  • Astro-turf pitch

    A true, even surface that's kind on the knees.
